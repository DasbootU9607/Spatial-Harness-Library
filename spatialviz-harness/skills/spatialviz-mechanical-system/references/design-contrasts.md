# Construction contrasts

Source IDs in this table have prefix `MentalAnimation/MechanicalSystem/`. The archive supplies the images, wording and keyed answers. The interpretations below describe how a new author can realize the tested relation with determinate constraints. Use the [per-source audit](audit.json) for ambiguous original views and [coverage](coverage.md) for the complete inventory.

| Official comparison | What the reader must determine | Construction decision |
|---|---|---|
| Level0/0–1 and Level0/2–3 | Motion of the planet and carrier when the ring or sun is fixed | Use the same two rolling contacts. Exchange the grounded member and driver. Solve both mesh equations in the carrier frame before reporting frame-relative spin and orbit. |
| Level0/22–23 and Level0/24–31 | Sense from a specified shaft end despite different bevel and crown shapes | Identify the common tangent of the active flanks. Draw both CW tangent arrows in a shared contact section. |
| Level0/34–35 and Level0/38–39 | What a pin transmits across a slot | The slot passes tangential sliding and constrains transverse velocity. A translating yoke and a pivoted rocker turn the same pin velocity into different outputs. Show the guide or pivot. |
| Level1/32–35 and Level1/36–39 | How signed profile changes determine the next body's motion | In loaded opposing cams the radial changes must sum to zero. At a shared guided groove pin the changes must agree. Indicate preload, the common displacement and the local slope. |

Original design opportunity: replace a single rigid connection with a bearing or conditional coupling, keeping adjacent components in place. Establish the released state explicitly. Test whether the observable changes or becomes indeterminate. A free body might continue moving under its initial conditions.
