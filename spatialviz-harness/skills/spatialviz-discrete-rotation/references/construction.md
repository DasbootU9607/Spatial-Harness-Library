# Discrete quarter-turn contract

Use a fixed Cartesian frame and an asymmetric six-cell figure. The generator
records its seed, option ordering, rendering parameters and source revision.
The verifier independently applies the rotation matrix and checks exact generator
replay, integer coordinates, unique options and the designated answer.

Rendering uses a fixed orthographic SVG layout, a black origin marker and visible
x/y axes. Preserve the figure's blue cells. The original is at left and four
choices at right. Inspect that labels, axes and cells are legible before delivery.

This is a labeled construction fixture, not an official question or a human
difficulty assessment. Its official reference association is discovery metadata
only; no official source image or answer is altered or relabeled.
