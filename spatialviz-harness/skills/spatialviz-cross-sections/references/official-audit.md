# CrossSection source audit

## Released materials

The official collection contains 120 `VisualPenetration/CrossSection/` source IDs. It uses the singular identifier `CrossSection`. All stems name the component order, show two gray views labeled View 1 and View 2, and ask which one of four line drawings cannot be a section. No released prompt identifies a single cutting plane, viewing arrow, camera movement for the solver, or restriction to three axis-aligned families. The answer is the letter of the incompatible drawing. Official labels, exact stems, explanations, media paths, dimensions and SHA-256 digests live in the [inventory](official-inventory.json). The [item-by-item readings](observations.md) link directly to every original PNG. The local originals in [representatives](representatives/) are unchanged bytes. Dataset revision `c722d00b2f740eacb1b56f4cbcd3af47bbbe40b8`.

Counts of valid choices overlap within items. Fifteen Level 2 items have one rotated witness, two items have two, and 23 have none. Witness frequencies are 8 about x through 45°, 4 about x through 135°, 5 about y through 45° and 2 about z through 45°. A z rotation changes vertical azimuth. An x/y rotation can cross different tiers at different offsets and cut a circular side obliquely.

## Native construction and composition

The pinned [CAD source](https://github.com/wangst0181/SpatialViz-Bench/blob/c27f9111a2674f2ea03e2ca1f5833326cf1eec1a/create_freecad/create_visual_penetration/create_cross_section.py) places horizontal tiers bottom-up, drawing from square prisms (code token `cube`), cylinders, triangular prisms, square/triangular/circular frusta, cones and square/triangular pyramids. A FreeCAD compound holds the constituent solids. CAD object interfaces survive in the section wires. Where two outlines coincide, the result looks like one line. The real images do not imply hollow shells or subtractive voids. An offset slice of a point contact can leave a gap between otherwise touching solids.

The first camera requests an axonometric view. The second multiplies the current orientation by a 0.7π z rotation. A panel is a rendering of the solid. Each lower option is a planar wire from `compound.slice`, displayed along its dedicated camera direction. YZ uses viewRight, XZ viewFront and XY viewTop. For each family the source cuts at one, two and three quarters of the model bounding interval. For diagonal candidates it chooses an axis, constructs 45° and 135° planes through the bounds center, aligns the wires, then uses a top, left or front camera respectively for x, y or z. A top face in the axonometric solid view is foreshortened; a horizontal section through a cylinder is circular when seen in its own plane.

To create the false picture the archived script rescales each tier with unequal base and height scale factors from 0.5, 1 and 2, realigns contacts, and cuts this modified compound centrally parallel to XZ or YZ. The [data driver](https://github.com/wangst0181/SpatialViz-Bench/blob/c27f9111a2674f2ea03e2ca1f5833326cf1eec1a/create_freecad/generate_data.py) shows a commented call to `prepare_composite_image_CrossSection`. The [compositor](https://github.com/wangst0181/SpatialViz-Bench/blob/c27f9111a2674f2ea03e2ca1f5833326cf1eec1a/create_freecad/visual_penetration_tasks_utils.py) crops blank margins, shuffles all valid sections, takes three, assigns shuffled labels and gives the remaining letter to the modified section. It prints the two references above A–D and saves the key and per-choice plane descriptions via `save_json`. The archive literally prints `Option {answer}` in the incompatible explanation. Released wording differs. Its procedure establishes provenance of its false picture but does not prove that the picture can never arise from some other plane.

An actual FreeCAD 1.0.2 run with seed 735 generated a three-component model, 11 genuine wire images, a resized-geometry distractor, an FCStd document, two views and a final annotated composite. The native model is retained in the F-drive delivery. The [output figure](upstream-replay/0-cylinder-cube-triangular_prism.png), [untouched annotation](upstream-replay/data.json) and [input/output fingerprints](upstream-replay/receipt.json) document the run. The host bound the document created by main to the shared `doc` used by helpers, saved the document before close, and substituted Arial Bold for the unavailable named font.

## Mechanisms and representative readings

- **Horizontal versus vertical.** In [cone on square frustum](representatives/Level0-0-cone-frustum_square.png), A is a circular XY cut of the cone and D a square XY cut of the support. B passes through both bodies off the cone axis, producing an arched cap with shoulders on the frustum. The axial-looking C puts the cone base outside the square support. The two source views show the opposite containment.
- **Preserved interface.** In [cone on cylinder](representatives/Level0-28-cone-cylinder.png), the base circle of the cone coincides with the top circle of the cylinder. The intersection of either with a nonhorizontal plane along that contact is the very same chord. A and D show that equality for an axial or off-axis cut. B can be a horizontal circle. C shows a narrow cylinder chord terminating inside a wider cone chord at their common seam, which that contact rules out.
- **Point contact and a missing tier.** In [pyramid on cone on triangular frustum](representatives/Level1-6-pyramid_square-cone-frustum_triangle.png), the cone touches the square-pyramid base at one point. An offset vertical plane can hit both solids separately and miss the contact point. C and D show a detached upper trapezoid over a curved cone chord. A misses the upper body altogether. B is the release's false option, with a deep roof and compressed bottom tier. The gap itself is valid. Twelve Level 1 items include two pointed components, a combination excluded by the archive's random object selector, which puts at most one pointed solid on top. Its named constructors accept such a stack when passed explicitly.
- **Oblique cylinder cut.** In [square pyramid on cylinder](representatives/Level2-3-pyramid_square-cylinder.png), B is the cylinder's circular horizontal slice. C combines a triangle and rectangle in an XZ plane. The released D witness is oblique at 45° about y. It cuts a round side below the join and the sloping roof above, so the curved lower boundary is valid. A shrinks the pointed footprint against a wide, deep rectangle; the original annotation assigns A without supplying dimensions or a general all-plane proof.

## Inspection and limits

The contact plates below retain all six panels from every original, with margins removed and wire ink strengthened for legibility. The inventory measures unmodified pixels. Its width/height ratio, long horizontal-line heights and four width samples describe a displayed outline. They are review aids, not physical dimensions or a universal solver. Cross-level shape repetition helps separate section logic from changes in proportions. Original records do not disclose their exact CAD dimensions, section offsets or per-item random seeds. Official keys are retained separately from local geometric readings. The authored marked-plane items add an explicit cutting line and viewing direction. Release-format items preserve the unrestricted-plane question and prove impossibility by comparing chords at a nested contact.

## Explicit point-contact replay

A second native run supplied the released shape names square pyramid, cone and triangular frustum to the archived constructors. This bypassed the random selection line and retained the FreeCAD slicing and compositing functions. [Offset XZ wire](upstream-replay/point-contact-offset.png) shows the truncated pyramid separated from a rounded off-axis cone chord, which still rests on the lower trapezoid. The unchanged original random selection and this explicit-shape replay each have a separate native document and recorder execution.
